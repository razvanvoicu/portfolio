A Quirky One...
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. role:: urcci-emph
   :class: urcci-emph

I like to exercise at home, and I rig my own gym equipment to increase the resistance. 
The result of that is that the embedded calorie counter becomes useless, 
because it is calibrated for the original resistance. A simple solution is to count
the number of repetitions and the elapsed time, and figure out a formula to estimate the calories burned.
How do you count the number of repetitions? This is what this app does.
:urcci-emph:`Point the camera at a repetitive motion, and it will count the number of repetitions.` 
It also allows you to input some parameters and use that in a formula to estimate the calories burned.
How do you figure out the values of those parameters? I have a few articles about that...

While one phone is pointing at the repetitive motion, 
you can use another phone to open this app and see the count in real time.
For that to work, you need to log in with a Google account.

This app could be useful to you if...

* You don't trust your equipment's embedded calorie counter, 
  and you want to estimate the calories burned based on your own formula.
* You want to keep a unified history of your workouts across multiple pieces of equipment.
* Your calorie counter is broken, and you don't want to replace your equipment just for that.
* Like me, you like to rig/customize your own equipment, and you need a flexible way to estimate the calories burned.
* **You want to see how much computation can be done inside a browser.**

Yes, that is correct. This app runs entirely in your (phone's) browser. 
It also runs on a laptop, and you can use a laptop's camera to count reps if you want, 
and have a convenient way to place your laptop in front of the repetitive motion.

Why would one do that? 

* It reduces cloud costs (less to pay for me -- it's a free app, after all).
* No installation.
* Automatic updates (no need to check for updates, or install them).
