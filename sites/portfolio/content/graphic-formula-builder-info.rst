Build a formula from blocks, see the answer as you go
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Ever wanted to try a formula without writing any code or fighting with parentheses?

Drag blocks into the editor to snap a formula together: numbers, arithmetic, powers and roots,
trigonometry, logarithms, comparisons, and even if-then-else. Blocks only fit where they make
sense, so you can't build something broken. As soon as the formula is complete, the app shows its
value. There is no calculate button; the answer is always up to date, and Share copies the formula
as JavaScript, such as ``x > 0 ? x * 2 : -x``.

Add your own variables, give each one a name and a value, and change those values whenever you
like. The result follows instantly. Build pieces of a formula separately, drop the ones you want
to keep in the stash, and tap one later to bring it back as a second formula. Drop a block on the
trash to delete it.

Already have a formula as JavaScript? Paste it into the import view and it turns into blocks.

Press the graph button to plot the formula. Pick which variable to vary and over what range, and
the app draws the curve, ignoring that variable's own value while the other variables keep theirs.
Its Share button copies the range, the formula, the variable on the X axis and all the variables
as JSON. The graph view can also import such a graph: the formula in the editor goes to the stash, and the
variables are merged with yours, so nothing is lost.

Everything you enter is saved in your browser, so you can close the app and come back to the same
place. Everything happens on your device. No data leaves your phone or computer, and you don't
need an account.
