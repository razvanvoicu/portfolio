Don't count, just follow the cadence
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Instead of counting your reps, set a cadence and have the reps counted for you.
This app will let you set a target number of reps, and a cadence (in seconds) for each rep.
The app will then give you visual and audio cues to keep your reps in sync with the cadence.
It will also count the reps for you, so you'll know when to stop.
