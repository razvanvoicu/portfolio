Press a button, increment by 1
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Have you ever had to count people going through a gate? Or how many times something happens? Did you lose count?

This simple app will help you keep track of a count. 
It has a big button that is easy to press, and it will show how many times you pressed it.
You can reset the count to zero, or input any initial value you want (even negative numbers). 
The count is saved in your browser, so you can close the app and come back later to see the same count.
Everything happens in your browser. No data leaves your phone or computer. You don't need an account, 
and you don't need to sign in. Just open the app and start counting.
