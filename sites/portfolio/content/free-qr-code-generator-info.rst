Free, No Ads
~~~~~~~~~~~~

QR codes are so pervasive these days, and there are many QR code generators out there.
In spite of many free, open source QR generator libraries being available,
I am yet to find one (except for my own) that is completely free, has no ads, 
and does not require an account or sign-in.

I completed this one in less than 1 hour, so I can only release it for free. Enjoy!
