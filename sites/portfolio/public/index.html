A Quirky One...

I like to exercise at home, and I rig my own gym equipment to increase the resistance. The result of that is that the embedded calorie counter becomes useless, because it is calibrated for the original resistance. A simple solution is to count the number of repetitions and the elapsed time, and figure out a formula to estimate the calories burned. How do you count the number of repetitions? This is what this app does. Point the camera at a repetitive motion, and it will count the number of repetitions. It also allows you to input some parameters and use that in a formula to estimate the calories burned. How do you figure out the values of those parameters? I have a few articles about that...

While one phone is pointing at the repetitive motion, you can use another phone to open this app and see the count in real time. For that to work, you need to log in with a Google account.

This app could be useful to you if...

  • You don't trust your equipment's embedded calorie counter, and you want to estimate the calories burned based on your own formula.

  • You want to keep a unified history of your workouts across multiple pieces of equipment.

  • Your calorie counter is broken, and you don't want to replace your equipment just for that.

  • Like me, you like to rig/customize your own equipment, and you need a flexible way to estimate the calories burned.

  • You want to see how much computation can be done inside a browser.

Yes, that is correct. This app runs entirely in your (phone's) browser. It also runs on a laptop, and you can use a laptop's camera to count reps if you want, and have a convenient way to place your laptop in front of the repetitive motion.

Why would one do that?

  • It reduces cloud costs (less to pay for me -- it's a free app, after all).

  • No installation.

  • Automatic updates (no need to check for updates, or install them).

Free, No Ads

QR codes are so pervasive these days, and there are many QR code generators out there. In spite of many free, open source QR generator libraries being available, I am yet to find one (except for my own) that is completely free, has no ads, and does not require an account or sign-in.

I completed this one in less than 1 hour, so I can only release it for free. Enjoy!

Press a button, increment by 1

Have you ever had to count people going through a gate? Or how many times something happens? Did you lose count?

This simple app will help you keep track of a count. It has a big button that is easy to press, and it will show how many times you pressed it. You can reset the count to zero, or input any initial value you want (even negative numbers). The count is saved in your browser, so you can close the app and come back later to see the same count. Everything happens in your browser. No data leaves your phone or computer. You don't need an account, and you don't need to sign in. Just open the app and start counting.
