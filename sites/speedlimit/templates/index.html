Coverage & phone setup

Limits are estimates from LTA traffic signs, not verified road matches. The default is 50 km/h. Signs encountered within 30 m are checked against your heading (±45°), including rechecking over the next 30 m travelled. A turn of at least 60° resets a normal sign limit. School-zone entry sets 40 km/h until an End of School Zone sign restores the preceding limit, including through turns and other speed signs inside the zone. Flashing-light conditions are not evaluated. Sound is disabled below a 60 km/h limit. Parallel roads, temporary limits and missing signs can affect estimates.

Keep this app visible with location enabled and media volume audible. Install from your browser menu (iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen). Browser restrictions can pause GPS or sound in the background. Screen wake requires browser support.

Your location stays on your phone. No location is uploaded.
