Encode and Share
================

Have you had to share a secret between phones with someone you trust?
Passwords are tedious to type, and we prefer to copy and paste them.
Did you end up sharing the secret over Whatsapp or email?
Did you feel pangs of worry that the secret might be intercepted?

Enter the Free QR Code Generator. It encodes your secret into a QR code,
displayed promptly on your screen. Your counterparty only needs to scan
it to get the secret. No typing, no copy and paste, no worries about interception.

.. container:: shots wide

   .. image:: /assets/qr-gen/demo.gif
      :align: center
      :alt: A password is typed into the text box one letter at a time, the Encode button is pressed, and a QR code appears below it.

The whole thing in one screen
-----------------------------

There is one text box and one button. Type or paste whatever you want to share,
a password, a Wi-Fi key, a link or a short note, and press Encode. The QR code
appears right below, in black and white with a generous white border, which is
what scanners like best. Point the other person's camera at your screen and the
text shows up on their phone, exactly as you typed it.

If you want to keep the code, the Download button saves it as a large PNG,
sharp enough to print.

Nothing leaves your device
--------------------------

For a tool meant for secrets, this is the part I care about most. The code is
generated by JavaScript running in your browser. The text you type is never sent
to a server, never stored, and never logged. There is nothing to sign up for,
no ads, and once the page has loaded it works offline, so you can even turn off
your Wi-Fi before typing the secret. Add it to your home screen and it opens
like any other app.

A few honest limits
-------------------

- A QR code holds a couple of thousand characters at most. If your text is too long,
  the app tells you so instead of drawing something unreadable.
- The code stays on your screen until you close it, so make sure nobody is
  looking over your shoulder. Scan it, then clear it.
- It shares a secret between two people standing next to each other. For anyone
  further away, you are back to the usual dilemma.
